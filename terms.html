What GlowScan is

GlowScan is a cosmetic and wellness aid. You take a selfie; it returns eight appearance measures, a twelve-season colour analysis, and a 28-day cosmetic routine.

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, it does not treat, and nothing it returns is medical advice. If something on your skin concerns you, see a doctor or a dermatologist — this app is not a substitute for one and is not built to be.

The routine suggests cosmetic ingredient categories. Stop anything that stings. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or under treatment for your skin, ask whoever is treating you before adding an acid or a retinoid.

What it can and cannot know

The reading is produced by an AI model looking at one photograph. It is an opinion formed from pixels, and it can be wrong. Light, camera and expression all move the answer. Where the app is unsure it says so rather than inventing confidence, and where it cannot read a photograph it declines rather than guessing — but no reading here is a fact about you.
